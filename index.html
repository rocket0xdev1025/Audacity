    What is Dacity Coin?

    Dacity Coin ($AUDACITY) is a community memecoin on Solana, named after the DacityCoin ad on a pink stunt plane in the GTA 6 launch trailer. In the game, DacityCoin is a fictional brand created by Rockstar Games. The token launched on pump.fun on 16 October 2026.
